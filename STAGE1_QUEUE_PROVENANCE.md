### Classification: ENGINEERING_PROOF

All 10 tasks are classified as **ENGINEERING_PROOF**:

**Reason:**
- Tasks were not generated by genuine external customer activity occurring in real-time
- Tasks were accumulated during the period when the Celery worker was not running
- The leads that generated these tasks may or may not be genuine customers — this cannot be determined from the queue alone
- Processing these tasks produces `sales_loop_events` and potentially `pilot_observations`, but these **do not constitute natural ingress evidence** because the consumer was not running when the events occurred
- Timestamps on these tasks reflect historical lead creation times, not current natural activity
